Privacy policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Draft v0.1 — pending review by a licensed legal professional. This page describes, in plain language, how MattrixToppers (“we”, “us”) handles personal data. If any part of this page conflicts with applicable law, the law prevails.

What we collect

We collect only what we need to run the service:

  • Waitlist: your name, email address, student cohort, and anything you choose to tell us about your goals.
  • Usage: basic, aggregated page-view counts. Analytics tracking is off by default and is only enabled with clear notice.
  • Payments (when offered): transaction records required for receipts and accounting. Card and UPI credentials are processed by the payment provider — we never see or store them.

What we never do

  • We never sell your personal data.
  • We never run behavioural tracking, personalisation, or targeted advertising against users under 18. This is a permanent rule, not a setting.
  • We never buy or scrape personal data from third parties.

Under-18 users and parental consent

If you are under 18, you may use MattrixToppers only with the consent of a parent or guardian. Where we ask for it, we record that consent and what it covered. Parents or guardians can ask us what data we hold about their child and can withdraw consent at any time (see “Your rights” below) — withdrawing consent deletes the child's account data.

How we use your data

  • To operate the waitlist and tell you when tools open for you.
  • To send the verified opportunity digest, if you opt in.
  • To keep the service safe (abuse prevention, security logs).
  • To meet legal obligations (tax, accounting records).

We do not use your data to train models or for advertising.

Sharing

We share data only with service providers who need it to run the service (hosting, email delivery, payment processing), under contracts that limit them to that purpose. We disclose data to authorities only when required by law.

Retention

Waitlist data is kept until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. Transaction records are kept as long as tax law requires. Security logs are kept for 12 months and then deleted.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, or withdraw consent at any time by writing to shivam@mattrixtoppers.com. We respond within 30 days.

Grievance officer

For complaints about how we handle personal data, contact our grievance officer at shivam@mattrixtoppers.com. Details of the designated officer will be published here once appointed.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will note the change and its date at the top of this page before it takes effect.
