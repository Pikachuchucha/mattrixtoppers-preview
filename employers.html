For employers

Hiring, run by hand. For one role at a time.

We're piloting a small hiring service for founders and small teams: tell us the role, we source and screen candidates with real evidence of ability, you interview the shortlist.

How the pilot works

Four steps. No fine print.

  1. 01

    Tell us the role

    One role, one brief: what needs doing, what good looks like, what you'll pay. If we can't run it well, we'll say so upfront.

  2. 02

    ₹3,000 deposit

    A deposit against the sourcing work — non-refundable once work begins, fully refundable if you cancel before or if we fail to deliver.

  3. 03

    We source & screen

    We find candidates from the student network, screen them against your brief with real tasks, and send you a shortlist with evidence — not CVs.

  4. 04

    You interview & decide

    You talk to the shortlist and decide. We don't take a cut of salaries and we don't lock you into anything.

What we won't sell you

No guaranteed hires. Ever.

Anyone selling guaranteed placements is selling something that doesn't exist. We sell careful sourcing work — and we'll tell you when a role isn't a fit for the pilot.
