Refund policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Draft v0.1 — pending review by a licensed legal professional. Short version: if we don't do the work, you get your money back. If you cancel before we start, you get your money back.

Employer pilot deposits (₹3,000)

Our hiring pilot requires a ₹3,000 deposit. The deposit is non-refundable once work begins — “work begins” means the first substantive sourcing work for your pilot, timestamped by email so there is never ambiguity about when the clock started.

You receive a full refund when:

  • You cancel before work begins — no questions, no deductions.
  • We fail to deliver what the pilot agreement promised, within the agreed timeline.

How refunds are paid

Refunds go back through the original payment method within 7 working days of approval. You receive a written confirmation and a receipt for every payment and every refund.

What this policy doesn't cover

  • It doesn't promise hiring outcomes — the deposit covers sourcing work, not guaranteed hires. We never sell guaranteed placements.
  • Disputes about whether work began are settled by the timestamped email record described above.

Ask for a refund

Write to shivam@mattrixtoppers.com with “Refund request” in the subject and your payment receipt. We confirm receipt within 2 working days.
