The standard

What “verified” means here.

Every listing we publish must carry all three — or it doesn't get published.

01

Method

How we checked it — spoke to the employer, confirmed the role and terms in writing, checked the company's registration.

02

Verifier

Who did the checking. A name, not a department.

03

Date

When it was verified. A listing verified six months ago is not a verified listing.
