For parents

What your child is signing up for.

Plain answers, no marketing. If anything here is unclear, email us — we'd rather answer twice than leave a doubt.

What this is

MattrixToppers helps students build practical skills, finish real projects, and find checked opportunities — internships, gigs, scholarships. It's a learning and career platform, not a social network.

Safety first, by design

No open messaging between students and strangers. Community discussions are moderated. Profiles are private by default. We never sell student data, and we run no behavioural tracking or targeted ads for under-18s.

No fake promises

We don't sell 'guaranteed placements' or charge students for jobs. Opportunities we haven't verified are labeled as unverified, and our scam checklist is free for everyone.

Under-18 accounts

Students under 18 join with parental consent. Parents can request a copy or deletion of their child's data at any time — email us and it's done.

What it costs

The core platform is free for students. If we ever introduce paid features, they'll be optional extras — never pay-to-get-hired.

Talk to us

Questions, concerns, or want your child's account removed? Email shivam@mattrixtoppers.com — a real person replies.
