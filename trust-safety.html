Trust & Safety

Trust is the product.

Student platforms have a credibility problem. Here's exactly how we handle verification, moderation, privacy, and reports — in public, so you can hold us to it.

Verification standard

Nothing gets called 'verified' without all three: the method used, the verifier's name, and the date. A check from six months ago doesn't count. Unverified listings are labeled as unverified — never silently.

Scam protection

Our scam checklist is free and public. The offer-check service verifies suspicious offers within 3 working days. We never charge students for any of this.

Community moderation

Discussions are moderated. Spam, abuse, and personal data get removed. There are no open DMs between students and strangers — community happens in moderated rooms.

Privacy

Profiles are private by default. We don't sell data, run no behavioural tracking or targeted ads for under-18s, and analytics (when enabled) is privacy-respecting. Full details in the privacy policy.

Under-18 safety

Under-18s join with parental consent. Extra safeguards apply to mentorship and any adult-to-student interaction — which we design to be structured and visible, never private and open-ended.

Reporting

See something wrong — a fake listing, abuse, a privacy concern? Email shivam@mattrixtoppers.com. Reports get read by a human and acted on. In the platform, use the Human Desk.

Report something

Fake listing, abuse, privacy concern — email us directly. In the platform, the Human Desk handles it with a 2-working-day reply promise.
